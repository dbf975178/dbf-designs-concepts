Privacy.

This is a concept site. Nothing you type here leaves your browser.

What we collect

Nothing. The email, sign-up, RSVP, volunteer and donation forms are demos. They check your entries and show a result, and they send no data anywhere.

What your browser stores

Your language and light or dark choice are saved in local storage on your device so the site remembers them. Clear your site data to remove them.

Third-party files

Fonts load from Google Fonts and icons from unpkg, so those services can see your IP address when the page loads. Yard sign images are made on your device.

A real campaign

A live campaign site would list its processors, retention periods and how to opt out. This page marks where that notice would go.
