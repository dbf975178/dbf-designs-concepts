The Ashcombe Plan

Every commitment below has a cost and a way to pay for it. Figures are proposed goals for this concept.

  • Costs down
  • Wages up
  • Care you can afford

Costs down

A family's budget breaks at rent, power, food and childcare. These four commitments go after each one, with a price tag and a plan to pay for it.

Rent

Cap yearly rent increases at 4 percent or inflation, whichever is lower, in buildings with more than 12 units.

Power

Freeze residential electric rates for two years while the state audits utility profits, and offer zero-interest rooftop solar loans.

Groceries

Open a price-gouging hotline and let the attorney general fine chains that raise prices during an emergency.

Childcare

Cap childcare at 7 percent of household income for families earning under 150 percent of the state median.

Wages up

Minimum wage

Raise it to $20 an hour by 2029, then index it to inflation so it never erodes again.

Wage theft

Create a wage theft unit with the power to recover stolen pay and collect double damages from repeat offenders.

Overtime

Extend overtime pay to salaried workers earning under $62,000 a year.

Apprenticeships

Fund 20,000 paid apprenticeships in the trades, clinics and transit by 2030.

Care you can afford

  • Clinics

    Open or reopen a community clinic in every county within four years.

  • Insulin and drugs

    Cap insulin at $35 a month and negotiate bulk prices for the ten most prescribed drugs.

  • Rural emergency rooms

    Keep every rural ER open with a stabilization fund, so no one drives more than 40 minutes to reach one.

  • Medical debt

    Bar hospitals from suing patients over bills under $5,000 and erase medical debt owed to the state.

Questions, answered.

Will this raise my taxes?

No. The plan is paid for by closing a corporate loophole and a surcharge on hospital executive pay.

How fast does the minimum wage rise?

In three steps: $16.50 in 2027, $18.00 in 2028 and $20.00 in 2029.

What about small businesses?

Businesses under 15 employees get a two-year phase-in and a payroll tax credit.
