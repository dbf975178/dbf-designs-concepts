Accessibility.

Everyone should be able to read the plan, make a sign and sign up to help.

What we aim for

We build to WCAG 2.2 level AA: readable contrast in light and dark modes, visible focus on every control and touch targets of at least 44 pixels.

Keyboard and screen readers

Every page has a skip link, landmarks and one main heading. Forms use visible labels and announce errors. Dialogs trap focus and close with Escape.

Motion

If your device asks for reduced motion, the swinging sign, drifting dots and page transitions turn off.
