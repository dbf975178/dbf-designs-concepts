Three priorities. Real first steps.

Each plan ends with commitments for Maya's first 100 days in office. Days count from swearing-in.

Safer streetsSmall businessHousing near transit

Safer streets for every walk to school.

Too many school crossings have no signal, no marked lane and no crossing guard. Maya will fix them block by block, starting with the ones parents already named.

Goal

A signal, beacon or crossing guard at every school crossing in Ward 4 by the end of her first year.

  1. By day14

    Walk every school route with Hollis Elementary and Eastside High parents, and publish the list of unsafe crossings.

  2. By day30

    Request block-by-block crash and near-miss data from Public Works and post it on this site.

  3. By day45

    File a council resolution to light and mark every crossing on the list.

  4. By day60

    Hold a public hearing on Linden Avenue speed limits at the Main Street Library.

  5. By day100

    Report back on which crossings are funded, which are scheduled and which are still waiting.

  • By day 20

    Meet 25 Main Street and Mill Road owners in their shops and list their three biggest permit delays.

  • By day 35

    Propose one permit liaison at City Hall with a 15-business-day answer target.

  • By day 60

    Call for a one-page checklist, in English and Spanish, for every common permit.

  • By day 75

    Ask the city to waive sidewalk-dining and sign fees for first-year businesses.

  • By day 100

    Publish a permit tracker so owners can see where their application stands.

Main Street should be easy to open.

Permits take too long and nobody owns the problem. Maya will make one person at City Hall answer for every application.

“I waited eleven weeks for a sign permit. Nobody could tell me who had my file.”

Marisol Quade, owner of a corner café (fictional)

More homes along the Route 12 line.

Homes within a ten-minute walk of the bus mean shorter commutes and lower costs. Maya wants them built with neighbors, not around them.

  1. By day 21

    Alder Flats

    Map every vacant or underused lot within a ten-minute walk of a Route 12 stop and share it for comment.

  2. By day 40

    Linden Avenue and Main

    Hold one housing walk per stop with renters, owners and landlords before any zoning vote.

  3. By day 60

    Hollis and Main

    Propose allowing small apartment buildings of up to six homes near Route 12 stops, with design standards neighbors help write.

  4. By day 80

    Rivergate Boulevard

    Ask the housing authority to set aside a share of new homes for Ward 4 renters at risk of displacement.

  5. By day 100

    Mill Road and Rivergate

    Publish a public tracker of every housing proposal in Ward 4, from first filing to final vote.

Questions neighbors ask.

Can the council really fix a crossing?

Yes. Council sets the Public Works budget and can order signals, paint and crossing guards. Maya will ask for a vote on every crossing on the list.

Who pays for the permit liaison?

The liaison is a reassignment inside the permits office, not a new department. Maya will ask for the cost estimate before any vote.

Will new homes change my block?

Only near Route 12 stops, and only after walks with neighbors. Design standards are written with residents, not handed down.

How do I reach Maya's office?

Use the forms on Get involved or come to a porch chat. A live campaign would list a phone line here.
