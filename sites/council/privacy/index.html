Privacy

What this site collects

Nothing. Every form on this site is a demonstration that runs in your browser. No name, email, address or payment detail is sent to a server or saved.

What stays in your browser

Your theme and language choices are saved in local storage on your device so the site remembers them. You can clear them in your browser settings.

Announcement and pop-up choices

If you close the announcement bar or the email pop-up, the site remembers that choice in local storage on your device so it does not ask again. Nothing about the choice leaves your browser.

Third-party files

Fonts load from Google Fonts and icons from unpkg. Those services can see your IP address when your browser requests the files. There are no analytics, ad trackers or cookies.
