Accessibility

Our goal

Every neighbor should be able to read this site, use its forms and find an event. The target is WCAG 2.2 level AA.

What is built in

Full keyboard use, visible focus, a skip link, labeled form fields with errors written next to each field, and touch targets of at least 44 pixels.

Light and dark themes, an English and Spanish toggle, and motion that stops when your device asks for reduced motion.

The ward map

Each neighborhood on the map is a button. Tab or use arrow keys to move between them, and press Enter to pin one. The same information appears as text beside the map.

Tell us about a barrier

This is a fictional concept, so there is no inbox to write to. On a live campaign, a real accessibility contact would be listed here.
