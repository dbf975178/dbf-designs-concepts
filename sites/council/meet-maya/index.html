Maya Linden, neighbor first.

Bookkeeper, bus rider, block captain. She is running because Ward 4 deserves a council member who picks up.

ML

Four blocks from her childhood porch.

Maya grew up on Linden Avenue, went to Hollis Elementary and still lives four blocks from home. She keeps books for about thirty Main Street shops, so she reads a budget the way most people read a menu.

She is running to make City Hall easier to reach: shorter permit waits, safer crossings and homes people who work here can afford.

How she got here.

  1. 2003

    Graduates from Eastside High and takes a first job at her aunt's hardware store on Main Street.

  2. 2007

    Finishes an accounting degree at Riverbend Community College while working nights.

  3. 2010

    Opens Linden Bookkeeping and starts keeping the books for Main Street shops.

  4. 2016

    Becomes treasurer of Linden Avenue Neighbors and wins a crosswalk at Linden and Hollis after two years of letters.

  5. 2020

    Runs a rent and utility help desk at the Main Street Library.

  6. 2023

    Joins the Route 12 Riders Council and helps get the Rivergate bus shelter built.

  7. 2026

    Files to run for City Council in Ward 4 Eastside.
