Short bio.

Maya Linden keeps the books for about thirty Main Street shops and rides the Route 12 bus to work. She grew up on Linden Avenue, served as treasurer of the Linden Avenue Neighbors block association and helped win a crosswalk at Linden and Hollis. She is running for City Council in Ward 4 Eastside.
