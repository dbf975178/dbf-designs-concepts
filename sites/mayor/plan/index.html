Questions we hear.

Will rent caps stop new building?

New buildings are exempt for their first 15 years, and we legalize more apartments near transit.

Who pays for child care?

A fee on luxury tower sales, not a tax on renters. Families under the income line pay nothing.

How soon will buses improve?

The first frequency upgrades start in year one. Bus lanes follow within 18 months.

Where can I read the full plan?

Ask for the printed plan at any event, or text your neighborhood captain.
