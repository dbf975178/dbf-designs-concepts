Privacy.

This site is a concept demo. It collects nothing.

What the forms do

Every form on this site is a demo. What you type stays in your browser and is never sent to a server, a campaign or anyone else.

What your browser remembers

Four choices are saved on your device so the site feels the same next time: your language, your light or dark theme, and whether you closed the top banner or the email pop-up. There are no cookies, no analytics and no ad trackers.

Outside services

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from unpkg. Those services see your IP address when the page loads. The Add to Google Calendar links open Google only if you click them.

A real campaign

A live campaign site would need its own policy covering donations, texting consent and voter data. This one is fictional and does not.
