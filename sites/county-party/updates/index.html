News and press

Four precincts still need a captain

Marrow Creek, Quarry Hill, Millstone, and Tanner's Ford have no captain yet. The committee opened applications this week.

Read the full release

Captains keep one precinct informed and organized. The next training is on December 12 at the Main Library, and the committee pairs every new captain with an experienced neighbor.

Monthly meetings continue at Union Hall

County meetings stay on Thursday evenings at Union Hall through February. Anyone who pays dues can vote.

Read the full release

Doors open at 6:30 PM and the agenda is posted on the calendar page. Interpretation in Spanish is available on request when you RSVP.

Committee releases the 2027 slate

Eight neighbors will run for county commission, sheriff, school board, state house, and circuit judge.

Read the full release

Each candidate profile lists priorities and endorsing groups. The committee will hold a public forum in December so voters can ask questions in person.

Fall registration drive reaches 312 neighbors

Volunteers staffed tables at the library, the winter market, and three fire stations. This is an example figure.

Read the full release

New registrants get a follow-up call to confirm their polling place. The next drive is a mail ballot request day on February 20.

Candidate forum set for December 10

School board and circuit judge candidates will answer questions from the audience at the Main Library.

Read the full release

Submit a question when you RSVP. A neighbor who is not on the ballot will moderate, and the forum will be interpreted into Spanish.

Statement on evening early voting hours

The committee asks the county clerk to keep the Main Library early voting site open until 8 PM on weekdays.

Read the full release

Evening hours help shift workers and parents vote without missing work. The committee will share the clerk's reply on this page.
