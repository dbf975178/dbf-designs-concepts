          Questions about giving

          Who can pay dues?

          Any Cedar County resident. Dues make you a voting member at county meetings.

          Can I give monthly?

          Yes. Choose Monthly on the donation tab and the summary shows your total per month.

          Is my gift tax deductible?

          Political contributions are generally not deductible. A real committee would show its disclosure language here.

          Do you store my card?

          This demo never asks for card numbers and saves nothing. A live form would be handled by a payment processor.

          How do I get a refund?

          Email the treasurer within thirty days. Contact details are on the Get involved page.
