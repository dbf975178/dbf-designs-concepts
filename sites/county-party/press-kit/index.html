Short bio

Cedar County Democrats is the county committee for neighbors who want local government that works. Eighteen precinct teams meet monthly and run voter registration, canvasses, and trainings.

The committee is chaired by Rosalind Okonjo and vice chaired by Pablo Ferreira. Both are fictional people for this concept.
