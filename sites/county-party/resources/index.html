How to register

You can register online or on paper. It takes about five minutes.

Example deadline: Tuesday, February 9, 2027
  1. Check that you qualifyYou must be a citizen, live in Cedar County, and be 18 by Election Day.
  2. Gather your detailsHave your driver's license or state ID number ready, or the last four digits of your Social Security number.
  3. Fill out the formUse the online form or pick up a paper form at the library. Write your address exactly as it appears on your mail.
  4. Submit it before the deadlinePaper forms must be received, not just mailed, by the deadline.
  5. Confirm in a weekCheck your registration to make sure it went through.

Check your registration

Do this once a year and again after you move or change your name.

Example deadline to fix a problem: Tuesday, February 9, 2027
  1. Open the state lookupIn a live site this links to your state's official lookup tool. Cedar County is fictional, so this demo does not link out.
  2. Enter your name and date of birthUse the name you registered with.
  3. Read the resultConfirm your address, party if listed, and polling place.
  4. Fix anything that is wrongSubmit a new registration form. Then use the precinct finder to see where you vote.

Request a mail ballot

Any registered voter in Cedar County can vote by mail. Ask early so there is time to return it.

Example deadline to request: Friday, February 26, 2027
  1. Fill out the request formOnline or on paper. You will need the same ID details as registration.
  2. Send it to the county clerkMail it, drop it at the clerk's office, or ask a captain to carry it for you.
  3. Watch for your ballotIt arrives with a return envelope. Read the instructions on the envelope before you mark anything.
  4. Sign, seal, and return itYour ballot must reach the clerk by 7 PM on Election Day, or you can drop it at any early voting site.

Early voting dates

Vote in person before Election Day, with no excuse needed.

Example window: Monday, February 22 to Friday, March 5, 2027
  1. Pick a site and timeAny early voting site works for any Cedar County voter.
  2. Bring IDPhoto ID is simplest. Poll workers can explain other options.
  3. Vote and get your stickerAllow about 20 minutes. Lines are shortest mid-morning on weekdays.
