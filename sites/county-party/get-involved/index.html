What a captain does

  • Know your neighborsWalk your precinct a few times a year and listen.
  • Keep the list currentTrack who moved, who needs a ballot, and who wants to help.
  • Run a small teamMeet monthly with five to ten neighbors from your precinct.
  • Report backBring what you hear to the county meeting so it shapes the work.
