Privacy

This is a concept site. It collects nothing.

What this site stores

Your language and light or dark choice are saved in your own browser so the site remembers them. Nothing leaves your device.

Forms

Every form on this site is a demonstration. When you press a button, the page checks your entries and shows a message. No name, email, ZIP code, or amount is sent, stored, or shared.

Analytics and cookies

There are no analytics, ad trackers, or cookies. Fonts and icons load from public content networks.

A live version

A real committee site would publish a full policy describing what it collects, why, and how to ask for deletion. This concept page shows where that policy would live.
