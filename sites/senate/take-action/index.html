Pick a shift. Start this week.

Knock a few doors, make a few calls, or write a few postcards. Every hour counts.

  • Knock on doors

    Walk your own neighborhood with a short script and a list. Training takes twenty minutes.

  • Make calls

    Phone bank from home or from a union hall. We provide the list and the script.

  • Write postcards

    Handwrite notes to voters. Good for evenings and for people who prefer not to talk on the phone.

  • Host a stop

    Invite neighbors for coffee. A staffer helps with the plan.
