Privacy

This is a concept website for a fictional candidate, built by DBF Designs for illustration. It is not a real campaign.

What happens to what you type

Every form on this site is a demonstration. Nothing you enter is sent to a server, saved to a database, or shared with anyone. It stays in your browser and disappears when you leave the page.

What this site stores

Your browser may remember two preferences on your device only: your language (English or Spanish) and your light or dark theme. You can clear them by clearing site data in your browser.

Third-party resources

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from unpkg. Those services may receive your IP address when the files load.

If this were a real campaign

A real campaign would state here how it collects, uses, and protects supporter information, and how you can opt out. Please do not enter real payment details anywhere on this site.
