Where Jonah stands.

Three priorities, each with a plan. Read what he will do and how he will pay for it.

  • Good jobs
  • Health care
  • Retirement

Good jobs that stay here.

Fair pay, real training, and work that is built in our counties.

$22an hour as a statewide wage floor by 2030, then tied to regional prices.Campaign proposal. Not a forecast.

  • A wage floor that keeps up

    Index the minimum wage to the cost of living in each region.

  • Paid apprenticeships

    Fund training in the trades, health care, and clean energy.

  • Make it here

    Favor federal contracts for companies that hire and build in-state.

  • The freedom to organize

    Make joining a union a real choice, with fast elections and real penalties for retaliation.

Care close to home.

Lower costs, keep clinics open, and make medicine affordable.

Lower what families pay

Jonah would cap out-of-pocket costs and let anyone buy into a public option.

  • Cap annual out-of-pocket spending on insurance plans
  • Create a public option available in every county
  • End surprise bills for emergency care

Keep rural clinics open

When a clinic closes, a county loses more than care. It loses a reason to stay.

  • Raise federal reimbursement for rural providers
  • Fund telehealth hubs at libraries and schools
  • Train nurses and aides where they live

Make medicine affordable

No one should ration insulin or skip a prescription to cover rent.

  • Cap insulin copays at $35 a month
  • Let Medicare negotiate more drug prices
  • Speed up approval of generic medicines

A lifetime of work should add up to a secure retirement.

Retire with dignity.

Will Jonah protect Social Security?

Yes. He opposes cuts to benefits and the age increases, and supports lifting the cap on taxable earnings so high earners contribute fairly.

What about pensions?

He backs stronger protections for workers whose companies fail, and a federal backstop so earned pensions are paid.

How does he help people who cannot save?

He supports a portable retirement account for workers whose employers offer no plan, with a public match for lower earners.

What about care for older adults?

He would expand home care so people can age in their own homes, and raise pay for the aides who provide it.
