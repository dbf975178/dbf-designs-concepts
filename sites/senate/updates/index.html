Nov 12, 2026Jonah calls for a wage floor that rises with the cost of livingStatementCampaign statement

At the Ironvale Trades Hall, Jonah Ellery outlined a proposal to tie the minimum wage to regional prices, starting with a statewide floor of $22 an hour by 2030.

“A raise should not need a miracle,” he said. “It should need a calendar.”
