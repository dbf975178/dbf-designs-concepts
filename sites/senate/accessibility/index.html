Accessibility

This concept site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. Accessibility is part of the design, not an afterthought.

What we built in

  • Every page works with a keyboard, with visible focus outlines.
  • Landmarks, headings, and a skip link help screen reader users move around.
  • Text and buttons meet AA contrast in light and dark mode.
  • Touch targets are at least 44 pixels.
  • Motion respects the reduced motion setting in your system.
  • The site is available in English and Spanish.

Known gaps

Some long-form content, such as event titles and news articles, is shown in English only. Validation messages follow your chosen language.

Tell us about a barrier

Because this is a fictional concept, there is no inbox. A real campaign would list a contact and a response time here.
