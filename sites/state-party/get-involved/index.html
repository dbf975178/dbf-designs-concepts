Questions, answered.

How much time do I need to give?

Start with one shift. Most canvass and phone bank shifts run two to three hours.

Do I need experience?

No. Every shift starts with a short training and a team lead.

Can I help if I am not registered?

Yes. Phone banking and event support are open to everyone. A captain can help you register.

Will I get a flood of messages?

No. Choose the channels you want when you sign up and change them any time.

Is there a path for students?

Yes. Campus chapters have their own organizers and meeting schedule.

Where do I go on the day?

Your confirmation lists the address and who to ask for. County offices open 30 minutes early.
