Leadership.

LV

Lucinda Voss-Adeyemi

State Chair

Lucinda Voss-Adeyemi has chaired the party since 2023. A former county clerk from Millrace County, she built the county committee program now running in all 24 counties.

TR

Tobias Reinholt-Nkemelu

Executive Director

Tobias Reinholt-Nkemelu leads the party staff and the statewide field program. He previously ran voter registration for a regional teachers association.
