Privacy.

This is a concept website. Every form, donation step and voter tool is a demonstration that runs in your browser.

What we collect

Nothing. Information you type into a form is not sent, stored on a server or shared with anyone.

What stays in your browser

We save your language and light or dark theme choice in your browser storage so the site remembers it. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Fonts and icons

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from unpkg, which see your IP address as any website host would.
