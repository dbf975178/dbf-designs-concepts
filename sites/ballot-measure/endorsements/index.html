Who backs Measure 12

Parents, teachers, shop owners, faith communities and workers. All fictional organizations for this concept.

Showing of

  • Parents

    Port Ellery Parents Network

    Every parent we survey has a story about a near miss on the walk to school. This measure answers it.

  • Parents

    Delaney Elementary PTA

    Our crossing guard counts more than 140 kids across Delaney Street each morning. They deserve a signal.

  • Parents

    Eastside Stroller Club

    We push strollers over cracked sidewalks every day. Fixing them helps parents, grandparents and anyone on wheels.

  • Parents

    Hollis Park Family Association

    Our park sits beside a four-lane road with no crossing. A marked crossing and a slower speed limit are overdue.

  • Parents

    Marlowe Street Walking School Bus

    Twenty-two kids walk together every morning. Safer corners let us invite more families.

  • Teachers

    Port Ellery Teachers Council

    Students cannot learn if they are late, frightened or hurt. Safe routes are part of the school day.

  • Teachers

    Bayview Middle School Faculty Senate

    We watch the corner outside our doors every afternoon. We know what it needs.

  • Teachers

    Cedar Lane Elementary Staff

    Our front office keeps a list of close calls. Measure 12 gives us a way to shorten it.

  • Teachers

    Port Ellery Retired Educators

    We taught thousands of children on these streets. We want the next generation to cross them safely.

  • Business

    Harbor Street Merchants Association

    Customers walk to our shops. Safer crossings and smoother sidewalks bring more of them through the door.

  • Business

    Tidewater Bakery Collective

    Our early shift walks to work in the dark. Better lighting and crossings matter to them.

  • Business

    Eastside Small Business Alliance

    Fixing streets once costs less than repairing them forever. We support a plan with a public audit.

  • Business

    Alder Row Cafe Owners

    Families stop for breakfast on the way to school. We want every one of them to arrive safely.

  • Faith

    Wren Hill Interfaith Council

    Our congregations share one concern: the children who cross Wren Hill Road. We ask for a Yes vote.

  • Faith

    Amity Street Congregation

    Many of our members walk with canes and walkers. Level sidewalks are a matter of dignity.

  • Faith

    Bayview Temple Social Action Committee

    Caring for neighbors includes caring for the street outside. We are knocking doors for Measure 12.

  • Faith

    Saint Odile Parish Council

    After Sunday services, families cross a busy road with no signal. A Yes vote changes that.

  • Labor

    Dockside Workers Union

    Our members build and repair this city. They also walk its streets to work and want them safe.

  • Labor

    Building Trades Council of Port Ellery

    Measure 12 means steady local work on crosswalks and sidewalks, done by trained crews at fair wages.

  • Labor

    Municipal Crossing Guards Association

    We stand in the road every school day. We know where it fails and we are asking for a fix.
