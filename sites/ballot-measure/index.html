What your Yes pays for

Crosswalks

Marked, lit crossings at the busiest school and transit corners, with signals where traffic is heaviest.

Slower school zones

Speed limits drop to 20 mph within a block of every school, backed by speed humps and clear signs.

Sidewalks that work

Cracked and missing sidewalk on routes to school gets rebuilt, worst blocks first.
