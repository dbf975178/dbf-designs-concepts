Ask us something

Thank you.

A volunteer will reply by email. Nothing was sent in this demo.

MythIt raises my property taxes.
FactIt does not. Measure 12 is a 0.12 percent sales tax for eight years. Property tax bills do not change.
MythCrosswalk paint does not stop crashes.
FactPaint alone helps little. That is why the measure pairs crossings with signals, curb extensions and slower speeds at the same corners.
MythThe money will vanish into the general fund.
FactRevenue goes into a separate street safety fund. By law it cannot be spent on anything else.
MythOnly school neighborhoods benefit.
FactSidewalks and signals serve seniors, wheelchair users and anyone walking to the bus. Projects are ranked by crash history, not by ZIP code.
MythLower speed limits just cause traffic.
FactSchool zones drop to 20 mph only within a block of each school during arrival and dismissal. Trips across town barely change.
MythNobody will watch how it is spent.
FactA citizens' panel reviews every project and publishes a yearly report. An independent audit is required every two years.
MythThe city can already afford this.
FactToday's budget covers about a tenth of the city's own repair backlog (example figure). Measure 12 funds the rest.
MythIt will take decades.
FactThe first 12 school crossings are scheduled within six months of passage, and the full plan finishes in eight years.
