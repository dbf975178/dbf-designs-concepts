Accessibility

Everyone should be able to read about, pledge for and vote on Measure 12.

Our target

We design to WCAG 2.2 level AA: readable contrast in light and dark modes, visible keyboard focus and buttons at least 44 pixels tall.

What is built in

Skip link, labeled forms with errors below each field, keyboard-operable menus, dialogs and accordions, and full English and Spanish text.

Motion respects your system setting. With reduced motion on, the stripes, counter and diagram appear without animation.
