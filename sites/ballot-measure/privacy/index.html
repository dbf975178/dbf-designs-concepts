Privacy

This is a concept site. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

What we collect

Nothing. Forms on this site show how a real campaign form would behave, then discard what you entered.

What stays in your browser

Your language and light or dark choice, and the example pledge counter, are saved in your browser storage so the site remembers them. You can clear them in your browser settings.

Third-party files

Fonts load from Google Fonts and icons load from unpkg. Those services may receive your IP address when they serve the files.

A real campaign

A real version of this site would publish a full policy covering email, text messages, donations and data sharing.
